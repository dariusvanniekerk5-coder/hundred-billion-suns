02 · narrative log

A voyage in four signals

LOG 01 · THE DEPARTURE

Every star begins as a question.

The Home Spiral turns beneath the survey craft. Eight voyagers leave the bright lanes, each carrying a different map of the same impossible question: how far can a story travel before it becomes light?

LOG 02 · THE ECHO

Something answers from the dark.

Beyond the halo, a second galaxy appears. Its signal is older than the mission and warmer than the void. The crew names it NGC VNK-02 and begins the long approach.

LOG 03 · THE BRIDGE

Gravity writes in luminous ink.

The galaxies meet. Stars are pulled into a tidal bridge, nebulae flare along the shear, and two histories begin to overlap. The collision is not an ending; it is a new geometry.

LOG 04 · THE AFTERLIGHT

What survives becomes a constellation.

Long after the first passage, the streams remain visible. Future voyagers will navigate by them, mistaking the scars of gravity for roads—and perhaps they will be right.
